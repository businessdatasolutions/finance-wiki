# Eric Ries

> Silicon Valley entrepreneur and author. Best known for *The Lean Startup* (2011), the methodology book that gave a generation of founders the validated-learning / minimum-viable-product / pivot-or-persevere vocabulary. **Promoted to a wiki entity on 2026-05-26** under the second-source author-promotion rule after appearing as the interview subject in two batch-co-ingested sources: [[2026-05-26-yc-2026-defend-against-mediocrity-and-rot]] (Y Combinator's *Main Function* podcast) and [[2026-05-26-lennys-2026-anthropic-costco-patagonia-incorruptible-companies]] (Lenny's Podcast). Both are book-launch interviews for his 2026 release *Incorruptible: Why Good Companies Go Bad and How Great Companies Stay Great*.

## What the wiki knows about Eric Ries

### From the 2026 interview pair (canonical wiki source material)

- **Author of *The Lean Startup* (2011)** — described by both interviewers as foundational to the post-2010 startup generation. Ries's own framing in the interviews: *"all my books come from pain... I never ask anybody to do something in a book that I haven't done myself."*
- **Author of *Incorruptible* (2026)** — the book the interviews are launching. Central thesis: shareholder primacy is a doctrine, not a law; companies that take it seriously systematically erode their missions; governance structures (PBC, mission-controlled boards, industrial foundations) can resist that erosion. See [[ries-2026-four-mission-protection-mechanisms]].
- **Self-described "incorruptibility" advocate.** Ries argues that the structural-governance question is more important than founder character because financial gravity will eventually exceed any individual's resolve.
- **Pre-Anthropic adviser.** Per the Lenny's interview opening: Anthropic's investors "suggested they come talk to me" before chatGPT had been invented; Ries advised Dario Amodei to write the long-term-benefit trust structure into Anthropic's charter at founding. The mechanism is now widely cited as the cleanest live example of mission-controlled-board governance.

### Public-record context (not verified against wiki sources)

- Co-founded IMVU (early 2000s) — the lean-startup-method's original test bed.
- Founded the **Long-Term Stock Exchange (LTSE)** in 2019 — a SEC-registered national securities exchange explicitly built for "long-term-oriented" companies, with listing standards that require commitment to stakeholders beyond shareholders. *(Wiki should not assert this beyond what its sources confirm; flagged here as public-record context that future ingests can substantiate.)*
- Coined and popularised: **MVP (minimum viable product)**, **validated learning**, **pivot vs persevere**, **vanity metrics vs actionable metrics**, **innovation accounting**.
