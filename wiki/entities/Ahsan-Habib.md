# Ahsan Habib

Professor at the **School of Accountancy, Massey University, Auckland, New Zealand**. First author of the canonical 1966–2017 literature review of financial-distress determinants and consequences — the wiki's foundational definitional reference.

## Role in the wiki

Habib appears across two sources in the 2026-05-25 ingest batch:

- **First author** of [[2020-01-01-habib-2020-distress-determinants-consequences-review]] — the comprehensive 53-page review synthesising the 1966–2017 empirical distress-prediction literature.
- **Cited as the foundational framework reference** in the opening sentence of [[2024-06-22-hajek-2024-distress-prediction-annual-reports|Hajek 2024]]: *"Financial distress can be caused by a combination of internal and external factors (Habib et al., 2020)."*

## Contributions

The 2020 review consolidates Habib's research programme on accounting consequences of distress (Habib, Bhuiyan, Islam 2013 on earnings management during the GFC) into a single field-defining synthesis. The review's central scaffold — **3 determinant buckets × 4 consequence buckets** — is the wiki's organising taxonomy for the distress literature.
