# Tables 1–2 — Demographic distribution of the 482-firm sample

> Sample composition for the US small-business distress study underlying [[bari-2026-hierarchical-regression-results]]. **N = 482 firms** spread across size, age, ownership type, industry, and US Census region categories. The sample skews toward small (≤50 employees: 80.3%), young-to-mid-age (≤20 years: 83.8%), and Southern US firms (33.6%). Industry distribution is broad — Professional Services (27.2%) leads, with Manufacturing/Construction/Retail/Hospitality each contributing 14–22%.

## Sample skew

- **Size:** 80.3% of firms are ≤50 employees — the sample is genuinely small-business focused, not SME-broad. The largest size cohort (50–249) is the smallest at 19.7%.
- **Age:** 83.8% of firms are ≤20 years old — the sample over-represents younger firms, which is appropriate for a small-business focus (older firms tend to grow beyond small-business size).
- **Ownership:** roughly balanced split between sole proprietorship (38.0%) and Corporation/LLC (39.4%), with partnership smaller (22.6%).
- **Industry:** the five categories are roughly balanced (14–27% each). Professional Services leads at 27.2%; Construction is smallest at 14.3%.
- **Region:** South dominates at 33.6%; Midwest is 24.5%; West and Northeast roughly tied at 22.0% and 19.9%. The South-heavy distribution is consistent with US Census Bureau small-business population data.

## Generalisability implications

The sample's composition is broadly representative of US small businesses for the indicator-family framework, but the geographic skew toward the South means findings may transfer less cleanly to:

- **High-cost-of-living regions** (West Coast, Northeast metro areas) where credit-behaviour profiles differ from Southern markets.
- **Non-US small-business contexts** — the regulatory and credit-access environment for US sole proprietorships differs from European SME contexts (where Altman 2023's Croatian SME sample lives).

The Discussion notes these scope limits but the regression itself does not test for regional moderators.
