## Variable key

X₁ = EBIT/TA; X₂ = S/TA; X₃ = NI/TA; X₄ = RE/TA; X₅ = EBITDA/TL; X₆ = WC/TA; X₇ = CA/TL; X₈ = CA/CL; X₉ = CL/TA; X₁₀ = NOCREDINT; X₁₁ = TL/TA; X₁₂ = FU/TL; X₁₃ = MVE/TC; X₁₄ = MVE/TL.

## Patterns

- **Indonesia & Philippines:** WC/TA (X₆) is consistently significant — liquidity dominates these distress signatures.
- **Malaysia:** RE/TA (X₄) appears in 5 of 7 periods — accumulated profitability is the dominant signal.
- **Singapore:** Most parsimonious models (often 2–3 variables); Singapore's AFC function failed stepwise selection entirely (no significant variables).
- **Thailand:** Highest variable count per function (5–6 variables) and broadest variable set across periods — Thai distress signatures are the *least* parsimonious in the sample.
- **Vietnam:** EBITDA/TL (X₅) appears in all 4 estimable functions with **negative** signs — Vietnamese distress shows a counterintuitive pattern that Powell notes but does not resolve.
