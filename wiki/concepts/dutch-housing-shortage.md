# Dutch housing shortage (woningtekort)

The structural undersupply of dwellings in the Netherlands — the cross-cutting policy variable that anchors the wiki's Dutch real-estate cluster. **Forecast: ~420,000 dwellings shortfall by 2026** ([[2025-12-18-rabobank-woningcorporaties-aan-hun-limiet|Cooiman & van der Zanden 2025]]).

## Magnitude

| Year | Woningtekort estimate |
|---|---:|
| 2025 | ~390,000 (implied) |
| 2026 | **~420,000** (RaboResearch forecast) |

Per [[2025-12-18-rabobank-woningcorporaties-aan-hun-limiet|Woningcorporaties report]]:
- 2012–2025: total housing stock grew **+12 %** (7.39 → 8.27 million dwellings).
- Same period: corporation-owned stock grew **+2.7 %** (2.27 → 2.33 million).
- Household growth +670,000 over decade — mostly single-person (+20 %).
- Annual new build 65–75,000 vs. annual household formation that exceeds this.

The shortage is not a temporary mismatch but a **structural backlog** widening year-on-year.

## Causes

The woningtekort is driven by a combination of supply-side and policy-side constraints — each documented across the 2026-05-25 ingest:

### Supply-side

- **Krappe arbeidsmarkt in construction** — 25 % of construction firms cite personnel shortage as #1 constraint (highest in 4 years), per [[2025-12-11-rabobank-sectorprognoses-2025-12|Sectorprognoses December 2025]].
- **Stikstof** policy bottleneck — Natura-2000 regulation constrains construction permits.
- **Netcongestie** — grid-connection delays for new dwellings.
- **Declining building permits** end of 2024 / early 2025 — feeds through to lower 2026 nieuwbouw deliveries.
- **Higher bouwkosten + rente** — squeezes investment economics.
- **Bouwlocatie tekort** — limited buildable land in densely-developed regions.

### Financing-side (corporation channel)

Per [[2025-12-18-rabobank-woningcorporaties-aan-hun-limiet|Woningcorporaties report]]:
- WSW guarantee volume crossed **€100 billion** in autumn 2025.
- **Vpb burden** projected to rise from ~€700 M to **€1.5 billion by 2027** under [[atad-interest-deductibility|ATAD]] interest-deductibility rules.
- Stricter huurregulering caps revenue.
- Verduurzaming verplichtingen compete with nieuwbouw for capital.

### Demand-side

- **Eenpersoonshuishoudens** rising (+20 % over decade) — each requires a separate dwelling.
- **Vergrijzing** — older households not vacating; demographic transitions constrained.
- Continued population growth.

## Policy responses

### Regiewet (effective 1 January 2026)

The **Wet versterking regie volkshuisvesting**. Per [[2025-12-18-rabobank-woningcorporaties-aan-hun-limiet|Woningcorporaties report]]:
- ≥30 % of all new construction must be **social housing**.
- ≥2/3 of new construction must be **affordable** (huur ≤ €1,300/month, koop ≤ €405,000).
- Rijk intervention powers to designate sites and accelerate permits.
- Municipal *voorkeursrecht* extended.

### Coalitieakkoord 2026–2030

Per [[2026-02-24-rabobank-vastgoed-selectief-investeren|Vastgoed 2026]]:
- More Rijksregie + middelen for woningbouw and gebiedsontwikkeling.
- Investments in defence, energy, infrastructure → indirectly supports housing supply via grid + transport.
- Overdrachtsbelasting drop from 10.4 → 8 % (1 Jan 2026), further to 7 % (1 Jan 2027) for non-owner-occupied housing.
- Beleggers re-engagement encouraged.

### "Beter benutten" strategy

Per [[2026-04-14-rabobank-beter-benutten-bestaande-bebouwing|Beter benutten report]]:

| Category | Mechanism | Target |
|---|---|---|
| **Woningdelen** | Friendscontracten, hospitaregeling | — |
| **Woningsplitsing** | Splitsgids, provincial subsidies | — |
| **Optoppen** | SFT scheme | 100,000 dwellings by 2030 |
| **Transformatie** | Kantoren/winkels → wonen | — |

The pivot from nieuwbouw to **beter benutten** is the supply-side response to nieuwbouw's structural constraints.

### Cooperative housing forms

Per [[2025-12-18-rabobank-woningcorporaties-aan-hun-limiet|Woningcorporaties report]]: *wooncoöperaties* as alternative organisational form combining lower per-household cost with social cohesion benefits.

## Debates and supersession

**Open debate**: whether Vpb / ATAD relief for corporations would unlock meaningful additional supply. Aedes-cited research suggests **+150,000 additional dwellings over 10 years** if Vpb were abolished — but this requires EU-law derogation (legally complex per [[2025-12-18-rabobank-woningcorporaties-aan-hun-limiet|Woningcorporaties report]]).

**Open debate**: whether "beter benutten" can realistically deliver 100,000 optop-dwellings by 2030. The Rabobank Beter benutten report itself flags that "in praktijk vallen aantallen tegen en er zijn nog flinke barrières."

**Open question**: scenario where corporaties "afhaken" entirely. The Woningcorporaties report quotes: *"Als corporaties afhaken, loopt de hele woningbouwketen vast, van gemeenten tot ontwikkelaars."* — i.e., the corporation channel is load-bearing for the entire pipeline; no clear substitute exists at scale.
